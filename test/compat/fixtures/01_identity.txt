-- v1 identity session (SPEC.md: Identity & auth) replayed as raw HTTP: the challenge/register
-- shape, me, subkey + revoke, JSON twins and the `err <code> <detail>` line format. v2 appends
-- fields after the v1 ones (challenge: for=; register: recovery= license= aup=; me: lvl= ipclass=
-- …), which the prefix comparison ignores. This is the fixture TestNegativePrefixLogicRemoved
-- replays byte-for-byte to show the suite fails without the prefix rule.

=== challenge
> POST /v1/challenge
< 200
c=$c bits=$bits:int exp=$exp:int

=== solve the proof of work locally
! solve nonce c bits

=== register
> POST /v1/register
> {"c":"$c","nonce":"$nonce","name":"compat-sub"}
< 201
id=$sub:id token=$tok_sub:token credits=100

=== a challenge is single-use
> POST /v1/register
> {"c":"$c","nonce":"$nonce","name":"compat-sub2"}
< 400
err pow

=== a forged challenge is refused
> POST /v1/register
> {"c":"not-a-challenge","nonce":"1","name":"compat-x"}
< 400
err pow

=== second challenge
> POST /v1/challenge
< 200
c=$c2 bits=$bits2:int exp=$exp2:int

=== solve
! solve nonce2 c2 bits2

=== a bad name is refused before the challenge is consumed
> POST /v1/register
> {"c":"$c2","nonce":"$nonce2","name":"bad name!"}
< 400

=== register a second root with it
> POST /v1/register
> {"c":"$c2","nonce":"$nonce2","name":"compat-two"}
< 201
id=$two:id token=$tok_two:token credits=100

=== me

=== subkey (credits move from the caller)

< 201
id=$child:id token=$tok_child:token credits=10 exp=$cexp:date

=== child identity

< 200
id=$child name=child root=$sub credits=10 rep=0 exp=$cexp

=== parent after the transfer
> GET /v1/me @$tok_sub
< 200
id=$sub name=compat-sub root=$sub credits=90 rep=0 exp=never
