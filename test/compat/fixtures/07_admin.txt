=== freeze writes

> {"what":"write","on":true}

=== writes are refused while frozen
> POST /v1/kb @$tok_f
> {"kind":"note","title":"frozen $r"}

> GET /v1/kb?k=1
< 200

=== unfreeze

> {"what":"write","on":false}
< 200

> POST /v1/kb @$tok_f
> {"kind":"note","title":"thawed $r","symptom":"after the freeze $r"}

ok $k:id
